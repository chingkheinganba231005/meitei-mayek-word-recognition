Handwritten Meitei Mayek word recognition

Write a whole word, as you would on paper: the recogniser reads it without cutting it into characters, and a character language model helps it choose between look-alikes. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you write or upload leaves your device.

One word, left to right, letters about a third as tall as the canvas. A pen about a tenth of the letter height reads best, as with a ballpoint on paper.

Dark ink on light paper works best. The word is cut out of the image automatically.
